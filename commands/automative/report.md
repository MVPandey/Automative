---
name: automative:report
description: Summarize the current (or a named) Automative run: baseline to best, keep rate, prediction accuracy, failures, strategies.
argument-hint: "[--run RUN_ID]"
---

Run `automative report $ARGUMENTS` and `automative ledger --last 20`. Then tell the human, in a few
plain sentences: what moved the metric, what did not and why, whether the predictions were close,
and what you would try next. Give the numbers. Do not restate every row.
